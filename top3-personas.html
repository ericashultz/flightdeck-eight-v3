Why they matter
Roughly 1,000 new users land on the hub every month with nowhere to start. The new onramp has to work for them, so test it with people seeing the hub for the first time.

Where you might find them

Start here
  • HR new hire roster, last 90 days
  • Onboarding & early career programs
  • Manager nominations, "who just joined your team?"
  • Hub analytics, brand new or never logged in accounts

Ideal fit

  • Under ~90 days at GE Aerospace
  • New to FLIGHT DECK
  • Will use the hub in their role

Probably not a fit

  • Tenured staff, even if new to a role
  • Anyone who already knows FD well
  • People who won't use the hub

Confirming fit

  1. How long have you been at GE Aerospace?Ideally · under ~90 days
  2. How much have you used the hub?Ideally · little or none
A note you're welcome to adapt · for the Newcomer

Because you recently joined, you have something we can't get from anyone else: a fresh pair of eyes. We're redesigning the FLIGHT DECK hub and want to watch a new person get started with no prep, there's nothing to prepare. Could you spare ~45 minutes the week of Jun 23? Whatever confuses you is exactly what we need to fix.
