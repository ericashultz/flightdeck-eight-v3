Wingmate Flight Prep

The platform evolved. So did the capability.

As Wingmate matured, it became clear that the underlying AI was capable of far more than coaching huddles. Today, Flight Prep prepares work before execution. Instead of supporting one workflow, it can create entirely new ones.

  • Generate action plans
  • Build worksheets
  • Create interactive tools
  • Produce calculators
  • Draft standard work
  • Generate checklists
  • Analyze photos and documents
  • Recommend next steps
  • Guide complex workflows
Flight Prep is where work is prepared before execution. Just as every flight begins with preparation, every task begins with Flight Prep.

One search,
across everything.

Today FLIGHT DECK carries two search boxes: the one SharePoint puts on every page, and the one that actually understands the hub. Only one of them survives.

Search

Built and running.

Type anywhere in the hub and results group themselves by what they are, with the term highlighted in every hit. Press enter and the full results page opens, styled to sit inside SharePoint.
