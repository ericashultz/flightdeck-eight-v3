Who it has to serve · your traffic, 2026

Two groups use the hub more than anyone else.

From the data Steven pulled. Everything after this is judged against these two.

Group one

Non-manufacturing individual contributor

“Show me what to do.”

Comes for the guides and the at-point help. Does not know the name of the thing yet.

Group two

FLIGHT DECK Leader

“I know what file I want.”

Arrives with a purpose. Wants the latest standard in a named template.

So the test for every option
  • Guidance for the one who does not know where to begin.
  • The fewest clicks for the one who does.
How we judge every optionCan one page guide someone who is new and still be fast for someone who is not?

Our recommendation

Option 02 serves both groups fastest.

  1. 1
    What changed is first. People come to the hub to see what is new, so it opens the page rather than closing it.
  2. 2
    Four choices, all built. The same four that are on every Fundamental page, so nobody has to learn anything new.
  3. 3
    Templates in two to three clicks. For the group that knows the file they want, nothing is in the way.

It asks nothing of GE that is not already happening, apart from keeping the announcements current.
