The Kaizen Journey

~25 weeks total: 12 weeks preparation → 5 days execution → 12 weeks sustainment

Essential Tools for Preparation

Complete these before the event week. Prep is where events are won or lost.

Week -12 | Required

Go/No-Go Checklist

Gates whether Kaizen is the right method. Confirms problem, KPI/TTI link, standard work exists, and team is ready. Requires 2nd Coach sign-off.

Download Template
Week -12 to +12 | Core

Event Checklist

Master task list with owners, dates, and template links. Drives all prep, execution, and sustainment. Dates auto-calculate from event Monday.

Download Template
Week -4 finalize | Core

Kaizen Charter

Source of truth: TAGS problem statement, KPI/TTI impact, genba scope, deliverables, SQDC+Cash objectives, team participants.

Download Template
Week -8 | Scoping

SIPOC

Define process Supplier → Input → Process → Output → Customer. Built in 3 iterations: fill process steps, go upstream, go downstream.

Download Template
Week -6 | Baseline

Process Map

Current-state visualization (swimlane for business, flow for manufacturing). Street-level view of sub-process with steps, decisions, delays.

Download Template
Week -6 | Manufacturing

Process at a Glance

Manufacturing process detail: C/T, material, tools, jigs, quality checks, poka-yoke, defects, downtime. Use flipchart, not digital.

Download Template
Leadership Kickoff Presentation

Executive Sponsor owns 4 touchpoints at weeks -12, -6, -4, -2

Spaghetti Chart

Maps physical movement of people/material to expose transport/motion waste

Safety & Ergonomics Assessment

Mandatory in prep and daily check. Heat map + power zone assessment

Supply Checklist

Event logistics: flipcharts, markers, tape, cameras, supplies

Essential Tools for the 5-Day Event

Change happens in the week. Monday through Friday, at genba, with the team.

Day 1-2 | Analyze

DOWNTIME Waste Guide

Identify 8 wastes: Defects, Over-production, Waiting, Non-utilized talent, Transportation, Inventory, Motion, Excess processing.

Download Guide
Day 3 | Generate

7-Ways

Generate minimum 7 distinct countermeasures per problem. Breaks conventional thinking. Creativity over capital.

Download Template
Day 3 | Prioritize

Impact Effort Matrix

Prioritize countermeasures: Quick Wins, Major Projects, Parked Ideas. Axes scaled to charter's KPI units to remove subjectivity.

Download Template
Daily | Visual Tracking

Kaizen Newspaper

Manual visual board tracking actions, owners, Harvey Balls status (25/50/75/100%), escalations. Don't digitize during event.

Download Template
Mon-Wed evenings | Leaders

4-Blocker

15-min daily debrief: KPI update, Accomplishments from Newspaper, Tonight & Tomorrow plans, Support Needed. Kaizen Leader runs.

Download Template
Day 4 | Lock It In

Standard Work Templates

Manufacturing: Time Measurement, Balance Chart, SW Combination Sheet. Business: SOP, Master Instruction, Detailed Job Instruction.

Download Set
Day 4 | Prepare to Sustain

Training Matrix

Harvey-Ball proficiency map: who can perform, who can train the new standard. Essential for sustainment.

Download Template
Value Add / Non-Value Add Classification

Mark steps Green (VA) / Red (NVA) through customer's eyes

Coaching Quality Workbook

1st/2nd Coach score Define/Analyze/Countermeasure/Sustain quality

Post-Kaizen Action Plan

Minimal residual actions, close within 2 weeks. If long, prep was weak.

Final Report Out Template

Friday presentation: before/after, KPI by day, lessons learned

Essential Tools for Sustainment

Without sustainment, improvements revert. 30/60/90-day reviews hold the gain.

Day 5 → 90 days | At Genba

Temporary Daily Management Board

Track leading indicator, training to standard, sustaining data & trend, Pareto, issue tracker. Used at genba until sustained, then transition to standing DM.

Download Template
30/60/90 days | Quality Check

Coaching Quality Workbook

Score Define/Analyze/Countermeasure/Sustain phases. Includes coaching criteria, questions guide, team retrospective, detailed job instructions.

Download Workbook
Week 2 post-event | Rollout

Standard Work Release Process

Define roles → Repository → Roll-out → Sustainment (30/60/90). Ensures new standard spreads and sticks across shifts and operators.
