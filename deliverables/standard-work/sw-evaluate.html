Evaluate or Update Standard Work

All functions~5 min checkSustain
← Standard Work

Check an existing standard against what good looks like, then revise it. The same six tests apply whether the standard is for manufacturing, transactional, or engineering work.

What good looks like

✓
Visible where the work happens. Posted at the point of use, not filed in a drive no one opens.
✓
Sequenced and timed to demand. Steps in real order with time values that hold against takt.
✓
Owner and sign-off named. One accountable owner, with safety and quality review on the sheet.
✓
Current with the process. Updated within a set window of any confirmed method change; old version retired.
✓
Followable on day one. A new teammate can run it without asking, in their own words.
✓
Reviewed on a rhythm. A recurring review is scheduled, not left to memory.

How to update it

  • Find the gap. Anything above you cannot check is the place to start.
  • Capture the current best way. Observe the work as it actually happens today, not the ideal.
  • Revise the standard. Generate a fresh draft, or open the template for your function and edit it.
  • Re-post and set a review date. Replace the old version everywhere and schedule the next check.
Worked example

A cell lead finds the grinder standard was never refreshed after a fixture change. They re-time the two affected steps, re-flag a safety point, re-post at the bench, and set a monthly review. The next operator follows the current method on day one.
