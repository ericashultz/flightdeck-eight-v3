Welcome

Thanks for helping us test.

You will look at two designs of the Standard Work page and tell us what you think. There are no wrong answers. We just want your honest reaction.

Before we start

How this works.

  • We will look at two designs of the Standard Work page, one at a time.
  • For each, you will try a quick task, then tell us what you think.
  • Please think out loud. Tell us what you expect, what you notice, what confuses you.
  • We are testing the designs, not you. If something is hard to use, that is on us.
  • At the end, you will pick the one you would rather use, and tell us why.
Warm-up · about 5 minutes

First, about your work.

Quick context before we look at anything. Keep it short.

  1. What is your role, and what team are you on?
  2. How often do you create, update, or look up a standard?
  3. When you need a template or an example today, how do you find it?Listen for: search, asking a coach, a bookmark, giving up.
  4. What is the most frustrating part of using Standard Work on the Hub right now?

Tell us why

The reasons behind the pick.

  1. What made your pick the winner for you?

Last things

Before we wrap.
