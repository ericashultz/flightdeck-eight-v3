How it works

From plain language to ready-to-use standard work

No searching for file names. No SharePoint browsing. Describe the process you need to standardize and Wingmate does the rest.

1

Ask in plain language

Describe what you need the way you would say it to a colleague. Partial phrases, job titles, process names, anything works. Wingmate understands context, not keywords.

2

Wingmate finds the right resources

It matches your request against the full Flight Deck library: templates, worked examples from real GE Aerospace functions, and the applicable steps from Define to Sustain.

3

Copy, adapt, and apply

Each result is editable and function-specific. Open the template, review the real example alongside it, then follow the steps. You are standardizing on day one, not after a week of searching.

🔎

This solves the findability problem

People know standard work exists but often cannot locate the right tool. Search fails on partial names, and a large library is hard to navigate. Wingmate removes the navigation problem entirely: you describe the work, it returns what you need, the template, a real example, and the steps to apply it.
