GE Aerospace Behavior

Continuous Improvement

We act with humility and are always in search of a better way. We learn from our shortcomings as much as we celebrate our wins. We embrace candor, sharing information to solve problems.

Understand the Behavior

Continuous Improvement Has Two Dimensions

The mindset

Kaizen (lowercase k)

Kaizen (lowercase k) is a culture and philosophy of continuous improvement. It’s the belief that small daily changes, made by everyone, everywhere, accumulate over time to create sustained competitive advantage.

“Everywhere, everyone, every day”

+
The standards

Kaizen Events (uppercase K)

Kaizen Events (uppercase K) are a structured method with standards for breakthrough improvement. These are facilitated process improvement events (typically week-long) with defined roles, coaching quality standards, and templates. Evolving formats include shorter kaizens and AI-powered hackathons.

Step-change SQDC improvement through structured events

Together: The continuous improvement mindset (daily improvements) and Kaizen Event standards (breakthrough events) create a complete continuous improvement system. One provides the cultural foundation, the other provides structured breakthroughs.

Learn the method

Cycle of Continuous Improvement

The philosophy behind everyday kaizen

Step 1

Value

  • Identify your customer
  • Determine their needs
  • Set Key Performance Indicators (KPIs) to measure progress towards goals
Step 2

Value Stream

  • Understand current state by going to genba
  • Identify activities that provide value to the customer and eliminate wasteful activities
  • Come up with an action plan for improvement
Step 3

Flow

  • Reduce uneven workload and overburden
  • Eliminate bottlenecks
  • Create Standard Work
Step 4

Pull

  • Provide products and services at the time they are needed
  • Wait to produce until the customer requests it
Step 5

Seeking Perfection

  • Regularly check progress against goals
  • Implement operating rhythms
  • Initiate problem-solving
  • Lead 5-Day Kaizen events for continuous improvement

… and align to our True North Metrics

Kaizen Events: Process Improvement with Standards

Structured, facilitated process improvement events (typically week-long) that deliver step-change SQDC improvement. These breakthrough events follow defined roles (Executive Sponsor, Kaizen Leader, Sub Leader, Facilitators, Coach), coaching quality standards, and templates. Formats are evolving to include shorter kaizens and AI-powered hackathons.
