GE Aerospace Behavior

Respect for People

We put safety first. We lead with transparency and value inclusive teams and diverse perspectives. We contribute to each other’s development in a constructive way.

Understand the Behavior

FLIGHT DECK Starts with Respect for People

Safe to Work. Safe to Speak Up.

Physical and psychological safety come first. Anyone can stop work when something seems wrong, and every near-miss should be reported. When issues happen, we fix the process - not blame people.

We Help Each Other Get Better.

Respect means being clear, giving honest feedback, and helping others grow. It also means providing the time, tools, and support people need to improve their work and succeed.

Those Closest to the Work Own It and Improve It.

The people doing the work know it best and should help shape how it gets done. That is why leaders go to genba to listen, learn, and support improvement where the work happens.

What it means: Employees have ownership of their work and are empowered to make improvements. Diverse perspectives are celebrated, contributing to creativity and better decisions. Leaders encourage scientific thinking: learning through experimentation, providing a safe space for innovation without fear of repercussions. Our organization thrives on open communication, continuous learning, and a shared commitment to mutual respect.
