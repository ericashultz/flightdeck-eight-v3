What this is. One document that answers the question behind the question: when will each page of the FLIGHT DECK Activation Hub be ready, who approves it, and what has to happen first. Phase 1 covers two deliverables. The first is the Dashboard, and the Dashboard is the homepage: the role based front door, the global navigation, the eight Fundamentals pages, and the three Behaviors pages, all one deliverable. The second is the Template Library.

How we work. Six weeks of design and testing, then two weeks of development. Every page moves through the same six steps: discuss, design, test, refine, approve, develop. Designs are tested with users as prototypes, refined, and approved in writing before anything is built. Prototypes are tested in weekly waves: the Template Library and Standard Work first, followed by the remaining Fundamentals, then the Behaviors, with the Dashboard and navigation last, once everything they point to exists. Standard Work is pulled into the first wave so its review lands Thu Jun 25 and refinements are in hand before the learning team publishes it Jul 1. Testing runs as the two streams GE asked for, moderated one to one sessions and async prototype review, against three validated personas. Each wave is tested the week after it is designed and shown live at that Thursday review. GE signs the design approval Fri Jul 17. Development is two weeks: every page is built in SharePoint Jul 20 to 24, tested Jul 27 to 30, and GE signs the final approval Fri Jul 31.

Phase 1 · Research, personas & what has to happen first

How we test, and with whom.

Testing runs as the two streams GE asked for. Moderated voice of customer sessions are one to one and observed live. Async prototype review lets the same users react on their own time. Both run against three validated personas so every page is tested by the people who will actually use it.

The three personas.

Validated Jun 17 – 18
Persona 01
Multiplier
Leads through others. Wants tools that scale a team, not just a task.
Persona 02
Newcomer
New to the system. Needs the front door to be obvious and forgiving.
Persona 03
Standardizer Leader
Owns the standard. Wants consistency, templates, and clear ownership.

Deliverable. A one page summary per persona, with the supporting survey comments behind each. Settled Jun 17 – 18 so testers can be matched to personas before the first wave.

Requirements

Laptops configured. The GE laptops, including Ken's, need to be delivered and configured so the team can work inside the GE environment. Target completion as soon as possible. How approval works. Every page is designed and tested as a prototype with users before anything is built, in weekly waves with revisions woven between them. GE signs the design approval Fri Jul 17. Development is two weeks: every page is built in SharePoint Jul 20 – 24 and tested Jul 27 – 30. Approval is written, per page, against the criteria in the project plan, signed together at the final approval on Fri Jul 31. Up to three revision rounds per page. A 30 day support window follows through Aug 28. Standard Work is the exception: it runs a week ahead, is reviewed Thu Jun 25, and is refined and published for the learning team by Jul 1, ahead of the Jul 31 approval.
