From signal to prototype

We built the hybrid.

Design A navigation as the spine. Design B onboarding when it’s needed. Recommended templates and Wingmate throughout. It’s live now.

flightdeck-bttr.vercel.app/deliverables/standard-work/sw-concept-hybrid.1.html
What makes it the hybrid
  • Navigate first. Function tabs and role cards put experienced users one click from their template.
  • Guided when needed. The four-move walkthrough expands inline, scoped to the function you picked.
  • Less to choose. One recommended template per function, not twenty.
  • AI throughout. Wingmate recommends, drafts, and evaluates at every step.
