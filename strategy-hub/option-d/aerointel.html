The Problem

Three structural pain points identified in how intelligence work is done today. AeroIntel is scoped directly to address these constraints.

01

Fragmented information

Insight work is spread across tools, teams, and format with no single, reliable place to research from.

02

Manual, slow research

High-value talent spends too much time on low-leverage data gathering instead of judgment and synthesis.

03

Inconsistent depth & quality

The depth and format of analysis varies by person and team.

Our Approach

The operating model: Align, Decide, Deliver

01

Align

Connected, cross functional teams and partners power a shared set of data and facts across the team.

02

Decide

Fast, trusted answers to complex questions with sources you can trust.

03

Deliver

Deep research grounds every analysis in curated, current export content.

Governance and Judgement

AI-generated analysis can materially speed up decision-making. It also carries known limitations that warrant continued human oversight, particularly for high-stakes or external-facing use.

Strengths

Breadth of synthesis.Draws on thousands of curated, expert-reviewed documents across domains and functions.
Consistency at scale.Applies the same logic and depth of analysis regardless of volume or time of day.
Speed.Reduces multi-day research and synthesis work to a single working session.
Traceability.Citations are preserved back to source.

Limitations to manage

Confidence is not accuracy.Outputs can be stated with certainty even when the underlying answer is wrong.
Sensitivity to framing.The same question, phrased differently, can return different quality of answer.
Bounded knowledge.Reflects training data plus curated content — not full real-time awareness.
Review is required.Outputs should be validated before use.
