Introduction

What Game Are We Playing (WGAWP) is the phase of the enterprise strategy process where opportunities are identified and framed. Strategy teams in each Business Unit select the topics worth pursuing, look at where customers, markets, and technologies are heading, and test the size of each opportunity against vetted market intelligence from AeroIntel. The phase sets the direction for every topic, so How Do We Win can convert it into an actionable business strategy.

Strategic Framework
