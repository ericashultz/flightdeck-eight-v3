Introduction

How Do We Win (HDWW) is the phase of the enterprise strategy process where strategic opportunities are converted into actionable business strategies. Building on the direction established during What Game Are We Playing (WGAWP), HDWW develops, refines, and validates strategic recommendations through collaboration across Business Unit leadership, functional teams, strategy, and SALT. The process produces a recommended strategic path supported by a clear business case, an implementation approach, and defined resource, investment, and execution requirements.

Strategic Framework

Each HDWW topic should be developed against the following strategic framework to ensure leaders have the information required to evaluate options, understand tradeoffs, select a path forward, and determine readiness to move toward execution.

03

01

Options & Evaluation

Lay out the strategic options for the topic and evaluate each one, so leaders can see the tradeoffs side by side.

02

Recommendation

Select the recommended strategic path, supported by a clear business case.

03

Implementation Plan

Define the implementation approach and the resource, investment, and execution requirements behind it.

04

Execution Readiness

Confirm the decisions, actions, and conditions required to close the strategy discussion and, where applicable, transition toward execution.
