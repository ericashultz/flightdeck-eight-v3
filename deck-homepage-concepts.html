Ready for departure

“Answer three questions.”

What it does
Three questions in a row, then a reader enters through the work they own. The eight Fundamentals, the tool library, how they connect, and the ten templates that do most of the work all sit below it.
Where it came from
The five concepts that follow. It takes the flow from 05, the entry by work rather than by role from 04, and the template shelf from 01.
Still open
It is at one of eight stages on the roadmap, with Fundamental Reviews next and publication on 13 October.

Concept 01

Stage first

“Where are you in your journey?”

What it does
Three stages down the page: get out of firefighting, tighten standards, then run the loops. A reader self-selects one.
What it buys
A reader who knows their own maturity lands on the right shelf in one click, and the page teaches the model while it routes.
What it costs
It asks for a judgement before it gives anything. A reader who does not know their stage has nothing to click.

Concept 02

Wingmate first

“Skip the search. Ask the question.”

What it does
The homepage is a prompt. Wingmate takes the question and routes, with four example asks underneath it.
What it buys
It uses the thing GE already trusts, the number one source outside the hub, and it needs no vocabulary from the reader.
What it costs
It bets the front door on one system. If Wingmate is slow or wrong, the homepage is slow or wrong.

Concept 03

Maturity first

“Show me where to focus this week.”

What it does
A maturity score against the peer group, a twelve-week trend, and the two Fundamentals that are moving.
What it buys
It answers the question a leader actually arrives with, and it earns a return visit because the number changes.
What it costs
It needs data GE has to supply and keep supplying. An empty or stale score is worse than no score.
Open the live page ↗
Concept 04

Role first

“Choose your starting point.”

What it does
Four routes by role, sized: Transformational Leaders around 400, Operating Leaders 285, and the coaches marked as the priority audience.
What it buys
Everyone knows their own role, so the sort works for every reader on the first visit with no learning.
What it costs
A person is more than one role, and the page makes them pick one. It also fixes the audience model into the design.
Open the live page ↗
Concept 05

Guided path

“The short flow below will help guide you to your next steps.”

What it does
Two questions, function then stage, and the page assembles the route from the answers.
What it buys
It carries the least assumed knowledge of the five and the answer is specific to the two things a reader can always state.
What it costs
It is two steps before any content, and a returning reader answers the same two questions every visit.

23 July · a different kind

Search first

“Type to jump anywhere, ask a question, or browse the whole hub below.”

What it does
A command palette over the real homepage. One field takes a search or a question, offers Search Flight Deck and Ask Wingmate as the first two rows, then lists all eight Fundamentals and the libraries underneath.
Why it is not a sixth layout
It is a navigation layer rather than a homepage. It sits on top of whatever the homepage is, so it can be added to what is live rather than replacing it. Its siblings are nav-concept-1, a consolidated bar, and nav-concept-2, a sidebar.
What it costs
A palette rewards the reader who already knows the name of the thing. Someone who does not is looking at an empty field, which is the same objection as concept 02.

Where we would go

The flow is the shape. Wingmate and search are the shortcut.

What is live already takes the flow from concept 05, which is the right shape for a first visit: it asks only for things a reader can state without being taught the model. The open question is what sits beside it for the reader who arrives knowing exactly what they want. Concept 02 answers that with a prompt, and Search first answers it with a palette that can be added over the homepage rather than replacing it.

Keep from 03
The score, once GE can keep it current. It is the only thing here that earns a second visit.
Keep from 04
The sizing. Knowing the coaches are the priority audience should shape the order, not the mechanism.
Open
Whether the homepage is allowed to ask anything at all before it shows something. That is a GE call.
