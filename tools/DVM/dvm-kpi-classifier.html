FLIGHT DECK Doctrine
Leading OR lagging with rapid resolution
Not every KPI on your board needs to be leading. A lagging indicator is valuable if the team can resolve the gap the same day it appears. The danger is a board full of lagging KPIs where problems compound before the team can act.
FLIGHT DECK Doctrine
The critical few, not the exhaustive many
Don't load the board with every leading metric you can find. Use the critical few KPIs the team can directly influence at genba — with fast visibility to gaps and same-day recovery actions.
FLIGHT DECK Doctrine
Three questions every DMS answers
1. Did we win today vs. target?
2. What factors caused the miss?
3. What are we doing now to recover at the root?
A board without leading indicators can only answer #1 — after it's too late.
FLIGHT DECK Doctrine
Run chart → Pareto → Problem Solving
KPIs that log a reason with every miss are Pareto-ready. That reason log, sorted by frequency, shows which root cause drives 80% of the problem. Rate-only KPIs can't do this — they give you a score, not a diagnosis.
