KPI Tree & Cadence Mapper

Map your team's KPIs from the top review down to the daily board, link each one to what it drives, and see which meeting reviews it. You can work through it together in about 20 minutes.

1. Set up your tiers and board letters

A tier is a level of review: a monthly business review (MOR), a weekly review (WOR), or a daily board. Start with the highest tier your team reports into and work down to the floor.
