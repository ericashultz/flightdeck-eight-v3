Metrics calculation

Three numbers turn a drawing into a diagnosis: lead time, flow efficiency and takt time.

Formulas Lead time = total time from start to finish, including waiting
Flow efficiency = (value-added time ÷ lead time) × 100%
Takt time = available time ÷ customer demand
