Who is FLIGHT DECK Accelerate For?

Whether you are new to FLIGHT DECK, have participated in a kaizen event, or are already helping your team activate the fundamentals, FLIGHT DECK Accelerate provides practical learning and resources to help turn knowledge into action.

What is it?

A flexible learning experience designed to help employees build understanding, strengthen capability, and apply FLIGHT DECK in meaningful ways.

How does it work?

Accelerate connects learning, reflection, and application over time through three pathways: Launch, Build, and Elevate.

You can follow the full experience from Launch to Build to Elevate. And if you're ready to take your FLIGHT DECK journey to the next level—or want to increase your impact—you can jump right into Build.

Launch Your FLIGHT DECK Journey Course

Launch Your FLIGHT DECK Journey helps build a strong foundation in our behaviors and fundamentals with FLIGHT DECK as the way we work at GE Aerospace. Whether you're new to FLIGHT DECK or already applying the fundamentals in daily work, you'll learn to spot waste faster, problem solve with clarity and create habits that deliver real results for our team and customers.

The course is offered both as a live facilitated session and a short, elearning option, so you can choose the style that works best for you.

Host a discussion with your team

You can also download a Team Discussion Guide to host a conversation with your team following the course.

What you'll get

  • An in-depth discussion of our Behaviors and Fundamentals.
