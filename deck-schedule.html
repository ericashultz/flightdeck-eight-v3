How one page gets published

Three weeks, start to live.

Every page follows exactly this path. GE reviews twice: one week with the Fundamental team, then 48 hours with Comms.

Week 1 · Monday or Wednesday

The Fundamental team reviews the page.

One full week to read it and leave comments in MarkUp. Reviews open two days apart, so no two pages start together.
