How it is different · 01

Search it,
or just describe it.

Know the term and you get structured results, grouped and highlighted across Fundamentals, templates, tools, and video. Do not know the term and you hand the same question to FLIGHT DECK Wingmate in plain English.

And when you do not choose, the box chooses for you.

How it is different · 02

It opens with
what you need today.

Every Fundamental opens the same way: a clear question and four ways to answer it. Then it refits around you. Manufacturing, non-manufacturing, or leader, and the examples, templates, and language change to match.

Templates sit inside the step that calls for them. Preview one in place, then take it. No hunting through a library for the thing the page just told you to use.

How it is different · 03

Coaching inside the page,
not beside it.

FLIGHT DECK Wingmate lives in the Fundamental rather than in a separate tool. Ask a question about the standard while you are standing in it, using prompts our own teams wrote.

It runs on approved internal channels, inside our own tenant. Nothing leaves.
