How it works

The whole thing in three steps.

STEP 1

You answer three questions.

Where you work. How your team is doing. What hurts most. Tap a pill for each — no typing, no wrong answers.

→
STEP 2

We look up your answer.

Every combination already has an answer written — twelve in all. Nothing is generated, guessed, or fetched.

→
STEP 3

You get one next step.

One recommendation, ready to run: send it to Wingmate as a prompt built from your picks — or, if you already know where you’re headed, open the destination yourself (the button names it, like “Open the Standard Work template”).

What it is

They don’t want a hub.
They want an answer.

One reusable component — the hero, the three questions, and the recommendation row — that turns the homepage into a triage. Three picks resolve to a single recommended next step: a ready-to-run Wingmate handoff, with a direct door for people who already know where they’re going.

The shape of it
1One component, two homes. Source of record on the hub home; standalone in the Tool Library as DB·01, loadable in the stage or full screen.
2Lowest-effort path. From “I landed on the homepage” to a Wingmate chat that already knows your function, stage, and challenge — or straight to the right page or template. No search, no navigation tree.
3Portable by construction. Self-contained HTML/CSS/JS, no framework, no build step — ready for the SharePoint tenant.

The three questions

Three questions. Only two decide where you go.

One pick per question. Only the last two change the answer — the first only changes the wording.

Q01

Where do you work?

Changes the words only
ManufacturingEngineeringHRSupply ChainDigital TechLeadership

Never changes where you're sent. Some picks add one sentence of wording — that’s all.

Q02

What best describes your team today?

Decides where you go
StabilizeStandardizeImprove

Picks the card under your branch. Stabilize, standardize, or improve — same challenge, different next step.

Q03

What’s your biggest challenge right now?

Decides where you go
Cadence driftDefect repairsOnramp confusionOther

Picks your branch of the tree. Three named problems plus Other — no answer dead-ends.

Acceptance criteria

Thirteen testable checks.

1Starts with <!doctype html> and includes /gate.js.
2Zero page and console errors on load — standalone and inside the Tool Library stage.
3No horizontal overflow at 1600, 1440, 1280, and 1024px.
4Default state shows the Manufacturing / Standardize / Cadence drift recommendation without interaction.
5Any pick updates every output — title, body, prompt, both CTA labels, both hrefs — matching the decision tree (verify 3+ combinations).
6The one-sentence rule holds: Engineering/HR/Digital Tech get the non-Mfg line; Leadership gets LSW; Manufacturing/Supply Chain get nothing.
7#0ad64f appears on exactly one element — the Wingmate handoff circle.
8Every destination href on the tree returns HTTP 200.
9Greeting matches local time of day.
10Library row DB·01 renders with its thumbnail; picks work inside the stage; tab filtering unaffected.
11Keyboard-only: every pill reachable and activatable; recommendation row reachable; visible focus ring throughout.
12With reduced motion enabled, content renders immediately with no rise animation.
13The handoff carries the full contract — ask, fn, stage, challenge, route — and the dashboard prefills the ask box from it, context attached.
